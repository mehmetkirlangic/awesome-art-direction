## Latest Updates

### November 2026
- [Nubank rebrands with Koto and a custom typeface, Nu Sans](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/24/nubank-rebrand-nu-sans/) - The bank's new type family grows from the looping curve where the n and u of its logo meet.
- [Google Search swaps its home page buttons for AI shortcuts](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/24/google-search-homepage-ai-redesign/) - Image creation, file questions and brainstorming now sit under the search bar on desktop.
- [Type Forward releases Salinger](https://typeforward.com/typefaces/salinger/) - A classical text serif with calligraphic touches, a flowing true italic and a variable version.
- [Docker redraws its Moby whale logo without the containers](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/23/docker-moby-whale-logo-redesign/) - Working with Motto, Docker drops the stacked boxes and gives the whale a rounder, simpler silhouette.
- [Claude mobile app moves Chat, Projects, Code and Artifacts into a bottom tab bar](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/23/claude-mobile-app-tab-bar-redesign/) - Anthropic's redesign puts each workspace one tap away at the bottom of the screen.
- [Contrast Foundry releases CoFo Friend](https://contrastfoundry.com/typeface/cofo-friend) - A neo-grotesque family built with interface and product typography in mind.
- [Pennsylvania Horticultural Society rebrands with a logo that grows like a plant](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/this-beautiful-logo-design-was-created-to-grow-like-nature) - Smith & Diction built a living, animated mark for the society's upcoming 200th anniversary.
- [Ava Studios turns viral launch videos into templates for your product](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/19/ava-studio-launch-videos/) - An agent plugin that copies the pacing and camera moves of a reference launch clip onto your own screens.
- [G-Type releases Victory](https://g-type.com/fonts/victory) - Nick Cooke's classical serif, drawn from HMS Victory's stern lettering, with high-contrast display cuts and a variable version.
- [The New York Times draws its 175th anniversary numerals from the masthead](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/18/new-york-times-175-anniversary-numerals/) - XYZ Type built the anniversary digits from strokes borrowed from the paper's blackletter logo.
- [Fuser lets you build custom editing tools from a sentence](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/18/fuser-tool-node/) - Describe a tool in plain words and get canvas controls you can reuse in later projects.
- [Apple names 45 finalists for the 2026 App Store Awards](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/19/apple-app-store-awards-2026-finalists/) - A closer look at standout finalist interfaces, from camera controls to Pencil-drawn mockups.
- [Maker's Mark rebrands with Turner Duckworth around its red wax seal](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/20/makers-mark-red-wax-brand-identity/) - The bourbon's dripping wax becomes a graphic system, paired with a custom typeface drawn from the co-founder's handwriting.
- [Figma AI now compares image models side by side](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/20/figma-ai-image-model-comparison/) - Generate several drafts from one prompt or test up to three models at once on the canvas.
- [Google Maps adds numbered pins for AI-planned itineraries](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/19/google-maps-numbered-itinerary-pins/) - Ask Maps builds day-by-day trips and shows the visit order with numbered map pins.
- [Benelli unveils a new brand identity at EICMA 2026](https://www.benelli.com/int-en/news/benelli-unveils-its-new-brand-identity-at-eicma-2026) - The motorcycle maker redraws its emblem, adds a standalone lion mark and a green, black and white palette.
- [Figma opens a 3D Transforms beta with CSS handoff](https://ux-news.com/figma-3d-transforms-beta-css/) - A new beta brings 3D layer transforms to the Figma canvas, with CSS output for developer handoff.
- [Production Type releases Vendôme Nouveau](https://typecache.com/news/7168/) - The Paris foundry revisits the classic Vendôme serif with a fresh contemporary family.
- [Crystal Ski rebrands with Landor around a snowflake-shaped "y"](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/18/crystal-ski-landor-rebrand/) - The ski travel brand's new identity turns a split letter in its wordmark into a snowflake lens.
- [Proof of Words releases PoW Dumbria](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/17/dumbria-wood-type/) - A display typeface by Reymund Schröder reviving the concave stroke ends of 19th-century wood type.
- [Unslop cleans up color, spacing and fonts on AI-generated screens](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/17/unslop-ai-ui-redesign/) - A Claude Code skill by Damir Sokolovsky that audits and fixes generic-looking AI web UIs.
- [Singapore refreshes its national brand around a glowing red sphere](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/17/singapore-brand-identity-refresh/) - The "little red dot" grows into a luminous sphere, carried by red gradients and light-and-shadow motion across campaigns.
- [LottieFiles launches Lottie AI for text-to-animation](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/16/lottie-ai-editable-vector-animation/) - Describe a movement in words and get an editable Lottie vector animation for loaders, icons or onboarding.
- [Google redesigns the Phone by Google app icon](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/17/google-phone-gradient-icon-redesign/) - A bigger handset glyph and a blue gradient lead a refresh of Phone, Messages and Contacts icons.
- [Caixa unveils its first logo refresh in nearly 30 years](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/this-banks-vibrant-new-logo-design-ticks-all-the-boxes) - Brazilian agency Tátil brightens the state bank's colours with a lowercase wordmark and a refined X.
- [Figma adds AI object selection and editable vector illustrations](https://designcompass.org/en/2026/11/10/figma-ai-object-selection-vector-illustration-editing/) - One-click object selection for images, plus agent-made icons that split into editable layers.
- [Commercial Type releases Schenk](https://typecache.com/news/7167/) - A new type family from Commercial Type, logged on the Typecache foundry release tracker.
- [Figma Config 2026 recap](https://dev.classmethod.jp/en/articles/figma-config-2026-recap/) - Figma Motion, generative plugins, shaders, Weave tools and the Figma agent, with Code Layers coming soon.
- [Figma Motion launch](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/6/figma-launches-a-new-integrated-motion-design-tool-alongside-code-layers-and-new-ai-features/) - Timeline animation built into Figma.
- [Framer 3.0](https://alternativeto.net/news/2026/6/framer-3-0-launches-ai-agents-branching-external-agents-and-a-redesigned-community/) - AI agents, branching and a redesigned community.
